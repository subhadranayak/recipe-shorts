Recipe Shorts — first time you open the app
===========================================

The app is not signed with a paid Apple / Microsoft certificate, so the
first time your computer may warn you. That is expected. The app only
runs on your computer and does not send your videos anywhere.

WINDOWS
-------
1. Unzip RecipeShorts-Windows.zip (right-click → Extract All).
2. Open the RecipeShorts folder and double-click RecipeShorts.exe.
3. If you see “Windows protected your PC”:
     click “More info”, then click “Run anyway”.
4. Your browser should open the tool. Keep the small Recipe Shorts
   window open while it works. Click Quit when you are finished.

MAC
---
1. Unzip the file (double-click the .zip).
2. You will see “Recipe Shorts”.
3. Do not double-click it the first time. Instead, right-click
   (or Control-click) the app, then click Open, then click Open again.
4. If macOS still blocks it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security,
   scroll down, and click “Open Anyway”.
5. Your browser should open the tool. Keep the small Recipe Shorts
   window open while it works. Click Quit when you are finished.

Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) Macs should use the Apple Silicon download.
Older Intel Macs should use the Intel download.

USING THE TOOL
--------------
Upload the original cooking video (not a copy downloaded from YouTube),
type the title, and click Make Short. If the video is 1080p or higher,
the Short is made at 1080×1920.
